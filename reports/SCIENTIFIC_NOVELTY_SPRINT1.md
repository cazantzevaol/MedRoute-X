Research snapshot: 27 October 2026. This is a targeted prior-art **scan**, NOT an exhaustive systematic review or patent search. Existing related work limits any “world-first” claim.

## Narrow defensible differentiator (hypothesis, not proven innovation)

> **A bounded, evidence-grounded inter-institutional medicine continuity sandbox** where independently scoped agents receive information asynchronously; a separate mathematical checker vetoes invalid plans; a conservative legal gate cannot be overridden by LLM output; and proposed domain-wide policy coordination gaps are distinguished from missing evidence and contrasted with different-jurisdiction practices.

This is a **combination and evaluation design**, not a claim that no existing system has these components. We have not established novelty against all papers, tools, vendor systems, or patents.

## Proposed falsifiable contributions for the full submission

1. **Asymmetric information experiment:** deliberately withhold a verified supplier constraint; measure how quickly/accurately the coordinator changes plan after disclosure, without leakage.
2. **Policy attribution experiment:** curated scenario labels from independent legal/pharmacy experts; score grounded, current-jurisdiction citations vs unsupported or out-of-jurisdiction assertions.
3. **Safety-gate ablation:** compare LLM-only, LLM + operational checker, and LLM + checker + conservative legal gate on identical scenarios, reporting valid, invalid, and abstained cases.
4. **Coordination benefit:** compare single-agent proposal, multiagent orchestrated proposal and same orchestrator with information boundaries. Record improvements *and* latency/cost and failure modes.

**Null hypothesis:** Adding agents does not improve safety, evidence correctness or coordination relative to a simple coded baseline. Our test design must be willing to retain this null hypothesis.

## Claims forbidden without new proof

- “First AI network for medicine shortage” / “unprecedented globally.”
- “Prevents patient deaths” or “clinically validated outcomes.”
- “Compliant with UAE medicine law” based on news notices or catalogue pages alone.
- “10 autonomous generative AI agents have been independently validated.”
- “100% legal accuracy” from the 8-case source-summary query set.

## Competition strategy

Treat the **policy/regulatory research engine** as the main paper contribution; the synthetic optimization problem supplies a concrete, testable use case. Additional real institutional and legal evidence is essential before strong claims about governance gaps.
